## 3. Replaceable Baseline Behavioral Design (`M6` Scope)

When decomposed into `.1`/`.2` `Active` requirements at `M6`, the baseline cartridge tracks localized physical knowledge carriers:

1. **Two-Track Representation (`ENG-RES-002`, `ENG-RES-003`):**
   - **Know-How (`TechComponentId`):** Modular working mechanisms and processes (pressure vessel, bored cylinder/piston, check valve, rotary crank, refractory lining, optical lens). Discovering or constructing one operational device (for example, a steam mine drainage pump) marks its constituent `TechComponentId`s physically *demonstrated* in that workshop/region, lowering trial-and-error difficulty for observers adapting those same components into rotary mill engines, river vessels, or locomotives.
   - **Know-Why (`TheoryDomainId`):** Abstract causal understanding generated by individuals liberated from full-time subsistence work (leisure thinkers, patronized scholars, universities, corporate/state R&D labs) studying natural phenomena and existing operational machines. Know-Why trails initial Know-How by decades, then multiplies directed R&D success probability and cuts material waste once established.
2. **Four Institutional Modes (`ENG-RES-004`):**
   - *Workplace/Leisure Tinkering* -> *State Prizes & War Arsenals* -> *Private Patents* (accelerating private investment while legally blocking competing domestic adaptation until patent expiry, reverse-engineering, or foreign leakage) -> *Universities, Corporate Labs & State Directed R&D*.
3. **Four-Carrier Fragility (`ENG-RES-005`):**
   Every settlement's capability for a `TechComponentId` or `ProcessId` queries four physical presence sets:
   1. Living practicing `DemographicCohort`s with experience above threshold
   2. Living `NamedIndividual` specialists carrying tacit design mastery
   3. Intact operational prototypes, precision tooling, or dies in `PhysicalLedgerPort`
   4. Undamaged written/printed archive volumes stored in local libraries/bureaus
   Plague, siege fire, or extended industrial shutdowns erasing all four carriers cause genuine capability regression.
